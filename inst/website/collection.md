---
title: "Data collection"
---

Observers visit the sites mid-monthly during the winter.
The season in Flanders ranges from October to March (6 months), in Brussels and Wallonia from November to February (4 months).
The observer surveys the entire site and counts the total number of birds per species.
The observer also notes which group of species they recorded.
Let's say the observer notes that they counted all ducks and waders and there is no count of northern pintail ([_Anas acuta_](../anas-acuta.html)) and no count of herring gull ([_Larus argentatus_](../larus-argentatus.html)).
The northern pintail is a duck, the observer counted all present ducks and did not report any northern pintail, hence the northern pintail was absent (or at least not detected) during the survey and thus a zero count.
The herring gull is a gull, the observer did not count any gulls, hence we have no information of the presence of the herring gull, which results in a missing count for herring gull in this survey.

Although the data collection started in the 1960's, the data collection was not standardized until the winter 1991-1992 for duck and geese and until the winter 1999-2000 for the gulls.
